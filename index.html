Start with a plan

Make your next build better.

Three simple principles to turn an empty patch of land into a memorable build.

01 / PLAN

Start with a silhouette.

Sketch the overall shape before adding small details. Strong outlines make builds easier to read from a distance.

02 / PALETTE

Limit your materials.

Choose a main block, a supporting block and one or two accent materials. Repeat them to create visual unity.

03 / DETAIL

Add depth and contrast.

Use stairs, slabs, walls, lighting and small changes in texture to keep large flat surfaces interesting.
